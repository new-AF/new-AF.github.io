Yes this is a Linux Operating System, there are others too and collectively are called "Linux Distributions". Debian is one such distribution, that is stable, has long-term support and is community-led

GNOME is a graphical interface/foundation for an entire Linux Operating System, it is the shape of the menus, buttons, the save file dialog, popups, etc.

Based on certain criteria: Yes

  • Debian is speedy, and bloat and ads-free, you get the full-power of your device without annoying background services degrading your experience and sending information back to Microsoft or whoever made the software you're running.
  • It respects your privacy: it does not periodically ping back any servers, unless you instruct it to, for example to download software.
  • It is 100% free, you don't have to worry about activations.

There's no catch, but there are downsides you should know:

  • The User Experience could be polished more, too many rough cases and small inconveniences that add up but considering you get full privacy and freedom that's a reasonable compromise, and things are improving.
  • There aren't as much software for Linux in general as is there for Windows, e.g. Office, or vendor-specific commercial software. but there are free and open-source alternatives that get the job done while being 100% free and respecting your privacy.
  • There's a higher learning curve: most things are expected to be done via command line Terminal instead of graphical interface, but things are improving.
